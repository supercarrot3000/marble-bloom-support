Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 September 2026

Marble Bloom is designed for children and deliberately limits data use. Advertising is contextual and is not used for behavioural profiling.

Information Marble Bloom uses

Marble Bloom uses device motion while the game is open so that tilting the iPhone or iPad can move the on-screen marbles. During ordinary production play, raw tilt readings are not intentionally retained or transmitted.

The game stores the player’s current board locally on the device so play can continue later. This local progress is not tied to an account or online profile.

Technical diagnostics

Marble Bloom sends a limited amount of technical reliability data to our own diagnostics service so we can identify crashes, regressions and performance problems. This can include product-interaction events, crash and error diagnostics, performance measurements, app version/build and platform information, and other coded diagnostic fields.

Each app run uses a new random technical session identifier. Marble Bloom does not use this as a persistent user or device identity. The client rejects common identity, location, advertising-ID and free-text fields. Production builds do not enable the high-rate tilt diagnostic stream by default.

Advertising

Marble Bloom uses Kidoz contextual interstitial advertising between completed boards. Advertising never interrupts active tilt gameplay.

Kidoz states that its Kids-category SDK does not contain IDFA and is intended for contextual, COPPA and GDPR-K compliant advertising. The KidozSDK 10.1.5 Apple privacy manifest included in Marble Bloom declares Advertising Data and Product Interaction. Kidoz declares both as not linked to the user and not used for tracking. Advertising Data is used for third-party and developer advertising; Product Interaction is used for analytics, advertising and app functionality.

Marble Bloom does not enable behavioural child profiling. The advertising integration is fail-closed and can be disabled remotely.

Personal information

Marble Bloom does not require an account and does not ask for a child’s name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or advertising identifier.

Sharing and sale of data

Marble Bloom does not sell personal data. Technical diagnostics are not provided to advertisers. Kidoz processes the advertising data described above to provide contextual advertising. Infrastructure providers may process network traffic as needed to deliver these services.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the app’s data practices materially change. The effective date above will be updated at the same time.
