Support

Marble Bloom is controlled by physically tilting an iPhone or iPad. If the marbles do not move, first check that Motion access is enabled for Marble Bloom in iOS Settings.

Before reporting a problem

  • Restart the current garden using the ↻ button.
  • Close and reopen the app.
  • Check that the device supports Motion and that Motion permission is enabled.
  • Include the device model, iOS/iPadOS version, garden number and what happened.

Open a support request

Do not include a child’s name, photos, location or other personal information in a support request.
